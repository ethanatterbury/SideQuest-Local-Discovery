# SideQuest

Stop scrolling. Go somewhere. A decision tool for people who want a worthwhile outing with very little research. Coverage priority is England; the currently prepared fallback still covers Surrey, Berkshire and Hampshire until a validated England dataset is published. Discover, Map and Plan my day share experience-based filtering. No account wall, no Supabase, no invented live facts. Deliver 3–5 considered choices or one confident SideQuest. Weather, daylight, time, travel, money, company and history influence decisions. Unknown hours and prices stay unknown. Estimates are explicit.
