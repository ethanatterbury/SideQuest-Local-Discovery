# SideQuest

Stop scrolling. Go somewhere. A local-first decision tool for people who want a worthwhile outing with very little research. Launch content covers Surrey, Berkshire and Hampshire; locations and providers are global models. No account wall, no Supabase, no invented live facts. Deliver 3–5 considered choices or one confident SideQuest. Weather, daylight, time, travel, money, company and history influence decisions. Unknown hours and prices stay unknown. Estimates are explicit.
