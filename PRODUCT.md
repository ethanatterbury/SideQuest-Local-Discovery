# SideQuest

Stop scrolling. Go somewhere. A decision tool for people who want a worthwhile outing with very little research. Coverage priority is England, with live area-based place searches as people browse and a compact local fallback for offline use. National venue catalogues and image collections are not bundled into deployments. Discover, Map and Plan my day share experience-based filtering. No account wall, no Supabase, no invented live facts. Deliver a short set of considered choices or one confident SideQuest, with more results available on request. Weather, daylight, time, travel, money, company and history influence decisions. Unknown hours and prices stay unknown. Estimates are explicit. Venue photographs load from verified external sources; browser retention stores links and attribution, not image bytes.
