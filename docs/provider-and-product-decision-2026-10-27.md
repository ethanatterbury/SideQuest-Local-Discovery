# SideQuest: places, photos and product replacement

**Superseded setup recommendation:** The owner subsequently ruled out billing and added `GEOAPIFY_API_KEY`. Geoapify is now integrated; the Google signup instructions below are historical and must not be followed for the current release. See [the no-billing assessment](apple-and-free-provider-assessment-2026-10-27.md) and [current setup](geoapify-setup.md). Local-venue photo coverage remains unresolved.

Decision recorded 27 October 2026. England is the first coverage target. The owner has authorised publication to main and production, but has not authorised enabling paid third-party billing. This document distinguishes the immediate bug fix from the replacement product.

## What the production checks established

The existing prepared catalogue has 7,180 regional places and 45 approved photographs, approximately 0.63%. More entries and a different card layout cannot solve that photo deficit. Read-only production API checks on 26 October returned zero places around Manchester and Newcastle, with live discovery unavailable. London returned 121 cached candidates and no photographs. These were candidate API responses, not proof that every candidate passed the UI filters.

The reported under-one bowling suggestion exposed a separate eligibility failure: family admission was being treated as age suitability. The immediate fix introduces an infant participation gate shared by Discover, map results and newly generated day plans. It also prevents old cached classifications from suppressing current bowling/mini-golf restriction checks. Ordinary museum, nature and animal visits remain possible, including venues with incidental playgrounds. Explicit play or cinema requests require positive infant-specific official evidence. This is conservative filtering; it does not establish stroller access, infant session availability or useful baby experiences for every venue.

## How to use the chosen supplier

Request places around the user's chosen location instead of bundling one starter area. Retrieve useful leisure/attraction categories in bounded requests and deduplicate branches. A country filter alone does not mean all England venues have been enumerated, and supplier popularity alone is not a SideQuest match.

Keep venue identity, primary experiences, secondary facilities, participation rules and evidence distinct. A play café can be a primary play experience even if its business category is café; a pub's incidental playground does not change its primary experience. Names alone are insufficient. Google's `goodForChildren` is likewise insufficient for infant participation.

Ruby's illustrates the required precision: its official site describes a play café, while its off-peak booking page gives different rules for 6–12 months and under six months, including a paying-sibling condition for the latter. It also describes upstairs/buggy restrictions. The provider's category or an attractive photo cannot replace these details. The future age model should use months for babies and represent participation versus accompanying another participant. [Official booking information](https://www.therubyplaycafe.co.uk/products/book-a-session).

For Google content, store permitted place IDs and the user's own plan data; refresh supplier details as required. Do not place Google results in the existing persistent OSM snapshot/cache, copy its photos to the permanent media catalogue, or put ordinary Google Places results on the MapLibre map. Photo names expire, and required author attribution must remain visible. Licensed first-party/open photographs can retain their separate storage path. A venue's public `og:image` can be a candidate, but public availability does not itself grant republication rights.

Select representative photos for the correct branch, favouring the actual experience over menus, logos and unrelated interiors. Load only visible-card images and bounded detail galleries. Use small honest missing-image rows when a real photo is unavailable; never substitute a different venue or generated venue photography. Validate chosen images visually rather than equating dimensions with quality.

## Replacement interface and recommendation contract

Build around three direct choices: Discover, Map, Plan my day. Put location, departure time, group/ages and travel budget near the top; progressively reveal detailed preferences. Preserve the planning concept, with travel, session availability, food opt-in and weather at the proposed visit time. The owner has rejected the prior mockups and current appearance; neither is the approved replacement.

Discover should show a small, strong set of real outings with representative photography, practical facts and evidence-based match reasons. Map should share exactly those eligibility rules, show recognisable category icons, clusters and a clear selected-place state, and avoid covering the geography with cards. Use a desktop list/map split and an accessible mobile sheet. Plan must never reintroduce a venue excluded by group, age or access requirements.

Eligibility comes before ranking: respect age/session/access restrictions, opt-in food/pubs/shops/fitness, opening status where known, travel and weather constraints. Then rank experience relevance, evidence quality, useful visit time, diversity and user preferences. Missing data is unknown, not a positive match. Distinguish "family visit" from "infant activity" in reasons. Do not claim a time-accurate itinerary from unverified opening/session information or estimated travel times.

Weather is a secondary enhancement to a responsive map. Clouds must evolve with time rather than camera displacement; precipitation must remain legible without obstructing place selection. Keep effects separate from map movement, bound rendering cost, support reduced motion, and judge the result on actual devices. A shader passing camera-invariance checks does not establish that its appearance is premium.

## What the owner needs to do

For the recommended Google-backed comparison:

1. Open [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) and create a project named SideQuest. Production photo access requires enabling billing; proceed only if this pay-as-you-go trade-off is acceptable.
2. Enable **Places API (New)**, **Maps JavaScript API** and **Places UI Kit** for that project, so the comparison can include both Google presentation options.
3. Under APIs & Services → Credentials, create two API keys. Restrict the browser key to Maps JavaScript API/Places UI Kit and website `https://sidequest-local-discovery.vercel.app/*`. Restrict the server key to Places API (New); do not impose a browser-referrer restriction on the server key.
4. In the existing SideQuest Vercel project, open Settings → Environment Variables. Store the server key as `GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY` and the browser key as `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY`. Do not paste the server key into chat. These names reserve configuration for the proposed integration; adding them alone will not switch this release's data provider.
5. Tell the assistant when the keys are stored. Before public activation, implement the provider/map/photo path, attribution, storage separation, API quotas and application request controls, then run the comparison below. Normal budget alerts alone do not enforce a spend limit.

If the strict-free requirement rules out billing, the alternative is one [Geoapify free account](https://www.geoapify.com/get-started-with-maps-api/), with its key stored in Vercel as `GEOAPIFY_API_KEY`. That provider is not wired into this release either. It can support a separately verified national listings path, with open/permissioned images, but it cannot honestly promise a photo for every place. Do not ask the owner to create several speculative accounts.

## Evidence required before declaring the replacement useful

Use the same real outing queries in London, Manchester, Birmingham, Newcastle, Bristol, Leeds, Norwich, Plymouth and rural areas in Cumbria/Cornwall. Include infants with month-specific rules, toddlers, older children, mixed ages, adults, rain, future departures and food off/on. Include independent play cafés, museums, parks, farms, animal experiences, bowling, climbing and swimming, not only famous city attractions.

For each provider record unique relevant venues, representative-photo hit rate, wrong-branch/irrelevant-image rate, duplicates, closed/private venues, age evidence, request latency and actual billable events. Inspect rendered photos and recommendations manually. Log counts/timing/cost locally without retaining supplier content beyond its terms. The product acceptance target is no unsupported age recommendations in this sample, at least 90% correct representative photos among the leading displayed recommendations, useful results across the named areas, and seamless desktop/phone interaction. These are proposed release gates, not achieved measurements or guarantees of completeness.

Publish progress to main as requested, but label interim fixes accurately. The infant bug fix is not the national/photo/UI replacement. No provider account was created, billing activated, England dataset acquired or Google integration shipped with this document.
